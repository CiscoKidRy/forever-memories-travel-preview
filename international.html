Passports, pacing, and a route that matches the group. International trips stay specific to the people traveling, not a generic tour. Forever Memories Travel plans them from Colorado Springs.

Plan my trip

The route follows the group

Write where you hope to go, or the kind of place if the city is still open. Then write who is going and how full the days should be. A first long trip and a return trip are different plans. Say which one this is if you know.

Passports and the days between flights

A passport that is current, and the time between long flights, belongs in the note when it changes the trip. We do not collect passport numbers on this page or in the form. Say only whether the passports are ready.

What the plan will not turn into

The plan stays specific to your group. It is not a tour with a fixed script. If you want one city and slow days, say that. If you want two cities, say that too. A family traveling together can start from the family vacation guide and name the countries in the same request.

A checklist before you write

  • Where you hope to go, even if it is still broad
  • Who is going
  • The month or the dates
  • How many places you want in one trip
  • Anything that changes the pace, such as a long flight or a first passport

If you are deciding what to write

Can the destination stay vague?

Yes. A region, or somewhere warm, is a start.

Do you need passport numbers?

No. Do not put a passport number in the form. Say only whether the passports are ready.

Is the request the booking?

No. The request starts the conversation. We shape the plan with you after that.
