The cruise, the cabin, and the ports.

We sort the cruise, the cabin style, and which ports are worth leaving the ship for. You get a week that is planned before you board. Forever Memories Travel does that planning from Colorado Springs.

Plan my trip

The ship and the week

A cruise can stand on its own. Write the week you hope to travel and who is in the cabin. A family, a couple, or friends can share that note. The cabin is a style, not a booking made on this page. Say if you want a window, a balcony, or the simplest room that sleeps the group.

The ports

Some ports are worth leaving the ship for. Some days are better on board. Write which you care about, or say you are not sure yet. We use that to shape the week with you.

A cruise after Orlando

A cruise can follow a few days in Orlando, or the park days can follow the ship. If you want both, put both in one request so the pace covers the gates and the day at sea. Read the Disney and Orlando guide if the parks are part of the same trip.

A checklist before you write

  • Who shares the cabin
  • The month or the dates
  • Cabin style, if you already know it
  • Ports you hope to leave the ship for
  • Whether Orlando days belong in the same trip

If you are deciding what to write

Do we have to name the ship?

No. A cruise and a season are enough to start.

Can the dates move?

Yes. Say the week you hope for, and what can move.

Where do we send this?

On the home page, in the trip form. Choose Cruise, or Not sure yet if the week might become something else.
